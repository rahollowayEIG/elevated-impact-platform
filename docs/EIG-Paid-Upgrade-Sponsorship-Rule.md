# Paid upgrade sponsorship reminder

Owner requirement recorded October 6, 2026. Applies across EIG/ElevationPilot whenever an organizer considers spending money on an upgrade.

## Required message

Ella, Marshal, and Maverick remind the organizer:

> Don't worry about the extra fees! We will show you how to get more sponsors to combat the cost of having an awesome event!

Show the characters and message in the upgrade review before purchase, alongside the actual incremental price. Apply this consistently to paid creative upgrades, additional AI generations, signage, banners, books, maps, venue layouts, and other event upgrades as those paid flows are introduced.

Include a **Help me find sponsors** action connected to the current event's Sponsor Builder. Preserve the organizer's draft and selected upgrade when opening sponsorship help. That help should carry the additional cost into planning suggested sponsor opportunities and packages; suggested sponsorship income remains a target until sponsors commit and pay.

## Current status

This is a recorded product requirement. Paid checkout, automatic AI generation, and the reminder itself have not been implemented or deployed by this documentation change. Apply the rule as those purchase flows are built.
